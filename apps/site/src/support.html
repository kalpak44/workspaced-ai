Common questions

How am I charged?+

You top up a prepaid balance in euros by card, through Stripe. Each running workspace is charged by the hour at the price shown when you created it. Stripe issues an invoice for every top-up, and you can download it from the Billing page.

What happens when my balance runs out?+

We warn you by notification in the week before. When the balance cannot pay the next hour, the workspace's machine is shut down and the workspace is paused. Topping up restarts it for a reactivation fee. A workspace still paused after three days is deleted with everything on it, and cannot be recovered.

Do you back up my workspaces?+

No. Deleting a workspace deletes its machine and disk, and an agent can delete files just as you can. Keep your code in a Git repository or another copy you control.

Who pays for the AI model?+

You do, directly. Each workspace uses your own OpenRouter API key, so model usage is billed by OpenRouter to your OpenRouter account and is not part of your workspaced-ai balance.

Can you see what is in my workspace?+

We do not look at your files, conversations or secrets in the normal course of running the service. See the Privacy policy for the narrow cases where we might.

How do I close my account?+

Email support@workspaced.online from the address we know you by. Closing an account deletes every workspace on it.
