Privacy policy

Draft · Last updated 19 November 2026

Draft, for information. This policy is a working draft published while Workspaced AI is in public beta, so you can see how we intend to handle personal data. It may change before our production launch. Questions: support@workspaced.online.

This policy explains what personal data Workspaced AI ("we", "us") collects when you use workspaced.online, ai.workspaced.online and related services (the "Service"), why, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It should be read with our Terms of service.

1. Who is responsible

Workspaced AI, reachable at support@workspaced.online, is the controller of the account, billing and technical data described in section 2. For personal data that you or an AI agent put into a Workspace (files, conversations, databases, secrets, published services), you are the controller and we act only as your processor, as set out in section 19 of the Terms. Contact us about privacy at support@workspaced.online.

2. What we collect

  • Account data - your email address, the first and last name you give when you sign up, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself) and any two-factor authentication factors you set up. These are held by our sign-in provider, Auth0 (section 5). Our own database keeps no email address, name or picture: only an internal account id, your Auth0 user id and your role.
  • Profile picture - we do not store one. The picture shown for your account comes from Auth0: the Gravatar for your email address if there is one, otherwise a picture of your initials. Showing it sends a hash of your email address, and the IP address of whoever is viewing it, to Gravatar (section 5).
  • Recipes - when you create a recipe, your first and last name as they are at that moment are stored with it and shown to every user who can see that recipe. They are deleted with the recipe, and your recipes are deleted with your account.
  • Billing data - your balance, top-ups, charges and invoices, and the identifier our payment provider gives your customer record. Card details are entered on our payment provider's pages and held by it; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Workspace metadata - workspace names, machine size, status, the names (not values) of your secrets and variables, firewall rules, connected domain names, and usage records needed to bill you.
  • Secrets - the values of your API keys and secrets, including your OpenRouter key, kept encrypted in a secrets store and delivered only to your own Workspace's machine.
  • Technical data - IP address, browser and device information, request logs, and security events generated when you use the Service.
  • Correspondence - what you send us by email, and our replies.

Workspace content - your files, conversations with the agent, terminal sessions and anything you run - is stored on the virtual machine of that Workspace. We do not access it in the normal course of running the Service. We may access it only when you ask us to for support, when strictly necessary to investigate a security incident or abuse, or when required by law.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To create and run your account and Workspaces, and to bill you - performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep invoices and accounting records - legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms and defend legal claims - our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To send you service notices, such as low-balance warnings and changes to our Terms - performance of contract and legitimate interests.
  • To answer your messages - legitimate interests, or steps you asked for before entering a contract.

We do not sell personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your Workspace content to train AI models.

4. Cookies

The app sets only strictly necessary cookies: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 12 hours, which scripts on the page cannot read; a security cookie that protects against cross-site request forgery; a cookie kept for 10 minutes while you sign in; and one kept for 30 minutes after you sign up without having verified your email address yet. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so no consent banner is needed. This website, workspaced.online, sets no cookies at all; it only remembers in your browser's local storage that you agreed to its privacy notice, so the notice is not shown again.

5. Who we share it with

We use these service providers (sub-processors), each bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Auth0, by Okta (European Union region) - sign-in and accounts: it holds your email address, hashed password, first and last name, and two-factor authentication factors.
  • Mailjet - sending the Service's emails, including the verification and password-reset emails Auth0 sends, from noreply@workspaced.online.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) - virtual machines that run your Workspaces, in data centres in the European Union.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) - network delivery, DNS, security filtering and email forwarding.
  • Our payment provider (European Union) - payments, saved cards and invoices.

Gravatar, a service of Automattic Inc. (United States), serves the profile pictures described in section 2. Your browser loads the picture from it directly, sending a hash of the account's email address and your IP address.

OpenRouter and AI model providers. Your Workspace sends prompts and context to the AI providers through your selected connector. Personal connectors use your own API key and provider account; the protected Trial connector uses a Workspaced-funded key with a $1 lifetime budget. What the providers do with that data is governed by the applicable provider terms, privacy policies and data settings, which you should review. The same applies to any other external service you or an agent connect to a Workspace.

We may also disclose data when required by law or a valid order of an authority, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or to a successor in a merger or acquisition, which will be bound by this policy.

6. International transfers

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, such as Cloudflare or Gravatar in the United States, the transfer is protected by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data and workspace metadata - while your account exists, and deleted when it is closed.
  • Recipes, with the author's name stored on them - until the recipe is deleted, or with your account.
  • Workspace content and secrets - deleted with the Workspace. There are no backups, so deletion is permanent.
  • Billing records and invoices - for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Technical logs - normally no longer than 90 days, unless needed to investigate a specific incident.
  • Correspondence - up to three years after our last exchange.

8. Security

Each Workspace runs on its own virtual machine with its own firewall, closed to the internet until you open a port. Traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit, passwords are entered only on Auth0's sign-in pages and kept there hashed, and secrets are kept encrypted in a dedicated store. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for the security of what you run and expose from your Workspaces.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. To use them, email support@workspaced.online from the address associated with your account; we will reply within one month. You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one in the EU Member State where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place. For data inside your Workspaces, contact the user who controls that Workspace.

10. Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page with its date, and we will tell you of material changes by email or in the app before they take effect.
