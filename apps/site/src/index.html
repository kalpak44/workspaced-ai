Recipes: share how you cooked it

Built something you love? A recipe is the story of how you made it, written so your AI, or anyone else's, can cook it again in their own workspace.

  • Cook. Ask your AI to build something, your way.
  • Write it down. Your AI turns what it did into a recipe you can edit.
  • Share. Publish it to the community, or keep it private.
  • Taste. Try other people's recipes and rate the ones you like.
