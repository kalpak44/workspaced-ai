Questions, answered

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You describe what you want in plain words and your AI does the building. If you do code, you can open a terminal and work alongside it.

Which AI can I use?+

Any model available on OpenRouter. You connect your own OpenRouter key and choose whichever model you like.

Is my work private?+

Yes. Every workspace is sandboxed on a machine of its own, and nothing is reachable from the internet until you choose to open it. Keep your own copy of anything important, as workspaces are not backed up.

What can I share with the community?+

Recipes: written-down ways to build something. You decide whether each one is public or private, and nothing in your workspace is shared unless you publish it.
