How the balance works

Is there a subscription?+

No. You top up a prepaid balance by card, from €5, and each workspace is charged by the hour at the price fixed when you created it. You get an invoice for every top-up. Automatic top-ups are there if you want them.

How much do I need to start?+

Enough for seven days of your workspaces, the new one included. For a Starter workspace that is €2.45, so a €10 top-up lasts about four weeks.

Does a workspace cost money while I am not using it?+

Yes. A workspace keeps its machine and disk for you, so it is charged for as long as it exists, used or not. Delete one you no longer need and its charges stop.

What happens when my balance runs out?+

We warn you in the week before. When the balance cannot pay the next hour, the workspace is paused. Topping up starts it again for a €5 reactivation fee. A workspace still paused after three days is deleted with everything on it.

What if a plan is sold out?+

Plans run on servers our provider sometimes sells out of for a few hours. While every plan is sold out, the app offers Starter Flex at €1.15 a day so you can still start; you can also wait for the cheaper plans to come back. Workspaces you already have keep running either way.
